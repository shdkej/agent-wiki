# 맥락이 목록보다 중요하다

A recurring principle that lists, entities, and static structure matter less than what is actively happening, why it is happening, and how context changes meaning.

## Why it matters
This idea connects dashboard design, agent participation, and knowledge organization.

An archive becomes more useful when it is reopened as a worktable rather than left as a warehouse. The point is not to keep adding items to a static list, but to spread out the capture, reclassify what changed, and pull reusable pieces back into active work.

When the material is mixed media, the visible surface has to come first. A photo set, a 9-frame storyboard, or a batch of captures is easier to reuse when the order is fixed before generation, not after it.

That applies especially to daily captures, image sets, and other mixed media inputs: first make the material visible, then decide what should stay raw, what should be promoted, and what should be sequenced into a next output.
